## Choosing printing process

Most well known and used:
* Offset Lithography: sheet fit machine, packaging. large runs.
* Flexography: transparent, labels, etc. large runs.
* Digital printing: like at home, short runs.
* Rotogravure: old news paper
* Screen printing

coated paper: bright
uncoated paper: matte
